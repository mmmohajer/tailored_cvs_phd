# Length

## Findings

- **There is no controlled study of PhD-applicant CV length in this review.** Industry one-page rules do not transfer. What universities publish is a range: about 1–2 pages for an early-career PhD applicant, with a third page only when the record or the funder allows it. A faculty CV that "can run to several pages" is a later document. The comparison is in `cv_structure.md`. [S18, D; S19, D; S20, D; S21, D; S28, D]
- **Statements are read at the decision stage, after a first cut that is mostly numbers.** In the 2015 CGS survey, doctoral programs named letters (67%) and the personal or research statement (64%) among the most important materials at the final decision, ahead of transcripts (55%). At the first screen, transcripts (71%) and GRE scores (62%) led. [S06, B]
- **A statement does not add much once grades and tests are known, if the outcome is grades.** Personal statements had small relationships with later grades and faculty ratings, and no incremental validity after standardized tests and prior grades. [S05, A]
- **Successful statements still have a recognizable shape, and it varies by field.** Background and reasons for applying are core moves; what gets emphasized changes with the discipline. The corpus is small and not PhD-only. [S10, C]
- **One faculty account of what the statement is for:** qualifications to start doctoral work, a focused research direction, and fit with the program or group. [S13, D]

## Reading the evidence together

S05 says a generic autobiography will not rescue a transcript. S06 says committees still weigh the statement when they are choosing among people who survived the first cut. The useful length is however long the program allows, filled with research preparation, a question, and fit. Padding to a page limit, and cutting a real project to look "concise" in the industry-resume sense, both miss the point.

## What the agent does

The program's stated limit wins: words, pages, characters, and spacing. Record that limit in `match_report.md`. When the program is silent, use the country file in `research/countries/` (a UK proposal is not a 700-word US statement; a German motivation letter is often 1–2 pages). The table below is the US default:

| Document | Default | Hard cap |
|---|---|---|
| Tailored academic CV | 2 pages (1 page is enough when the matching record is short) | 3 pages only when real publications or a long research history need the space, or when Germany's DAAD-like cap is the instruction. See `cv_structure.md` |
| Master CV (`master/master_resume.md`) | No limit | No limit. Tailoring cuts |
| Statement of purpose | 700–1,000 words | 2 pages in this renderer's single-spaced layout. Written when the page asks, or when a silent US, Canadian, or UK science page uses this as the country default |
| Cover letter | 250–400 words | One page, unless the position sets another limit. Always written. It does not replace the statement, proposal, or motivation letter |
| Professor email | 120–180 words | One professor. The agent does not send it |
| Follow-up plan | The send, one reminder, and the stop | One reminder after 10–14 days, then stop |
| Extra essay the program requires (personal history, research proposal, teaching) | The prompt's limit | The prompt's limit. A silent UK humanities page or a German individual route still gets a proposal. Do not add a second one the page did not ask for |

- Two or three CV pages is a ceiling, not a target. A short research record stays on two pages. Never shrink type below 10pt or margins under 0.5in to force a fit; cut the least relevant items.
- Page one of the CV carries education and the strongest research. Later pages hold older, thinner, or less related items.
- Do not pad a statement to reach 1,000 words. Do not write the one-page cover letter and call it the statement, the proposal, or the motivation letter.
- A careers page for academic jobs allows a letter of 1–3 pages and says not to retell the CV in date order. [S28, D] The cover letter here stays at one page and one proof story. The country essay is a separate file.
- A research proposal, writing sample, or diversity essay uses the program's length, not the statement default.
