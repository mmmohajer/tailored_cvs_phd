# Which country's rules apply

A strong PhD file is not one shape with the university's name swapped in. The same person can need a different CV, a different second document, and a different idea of "fit" in another country. National studies of admissions do not line up: US committees often cut on grades and tests and then read a statement [S06, B; S07, B]; at Oxford and Cambridge, humanities and social-science selectors treated the research proposal as the heart of the file, while science selectors treated the person and their skills as the heart and usually did not want a full proposal [S14, B].

## Findings

- **The documents look similar from a distance and are weighed differently up close.** Across the UK, US, Germany, and China, files tend to include transcripts, some form of statement or proposal, and references. Test scores matter more in some of those systems than others, and a proposal is decisive in some fields and rare in others. [S14, B, reviewing that literature]
- **Entry level differs.** In a four-university comparison of biomedical programs, US students commonly entered after a bachelor's degree, into an umbrella program with coursework before a lab was chosen. European programs in that comparison treated a master's, or a long combined degree, as the usual preparation for the doctorate. [S15, B]
- **Who decides differs.** Some systems admit a cohort and match a supervisor later. Others will not read a file until a professor has agreed to supervise, or until the applicant has applied to an advertised project. See the country files. Where this folder has no country file, use `unlisted.md`.

## What the agent does

Before drafting, name three things in `match_report.md` and in `master/profile_facts.md`:

1. **Country** of the university.
2. **Route**, from the saved page, not from the country stereotype:
   - **Committee:** apply to the program; a supervisor may come later.
   - **Supervisor-first:** a named professor has to agree, or an expression of interest comes before the formal form.
   - **Advertised post:** the doctorate is a vacancy on a defined project. The file is closer to an application for that job.
3. **Document set** the page asks for. That list wins over the country file. The country file is the default only when the page is silent.

Then read that country's file in this folder. Apply `research/fields/disciplinary_differences.md` inside the country, not instead of it. A UK science application and a UK history application are not the same file [S14, B]. A US science application and a UK science application are not the same file either.

Do not convert grades onto another country's scale. Do not add a photo, date of birth, marital status, or nationality unless the saved instructions ask for them. Do not send a US statement of purpose to a program that asked for a research proposal or a one-page motivation letter.

If the person is applying to several countries, tailor each one separately. Facts stay in the master. Shape does not.
