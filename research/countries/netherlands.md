# Netherlands

Dutch doctorates are often jobs. The metric is whether this person can do the advertised project, or whether a professor will host a project the applicant funds. A US statement of purpose aimed at a rotating cohort is the wrong document.

## Findings

- **Universities describe two routes.** Eindhoven distinguishes an employed candidate, who applies to a funded vacancy and becomes an employee, from a non-employed candidate, who needs their own funding, a professor's agreement, and a research proposal. A Dutch master's, or a foreign equivalent assessed per person, is the usual entry. English at C1 is required there. [S17, D]
- **Amsterdam's general guidance for finding a position asks for a motivation letter plus a detailed CV** (months, not just years), a publication list of at most one page if there is one, two academic referees with email addresses and the relationship, bachelor's and master's transcripts, and a sample of the applicant's own academic writing. [S17, D]
- These are two universities' pages, not a national audit. Other departments publish their own vacancy text. That text wins.

## What the agent does

The four files are always written: custom CV, cover letter, email to one professor, and a follow-up plan. On a vacancy, `motivation.md` is the country essay. With the applicant's own funding, `research_proposal.md` is the country essay. The cover letter does not replace either.

- **Vacancy.** Treat it as one project. `motivation.md` says why this project and which parts of the CV prove the methods. The CV uses months and years. Put the thesis title and the techniques on page one. There is no national page cap in this folder. When the vacancy is silent, the CV is 1–2 pages, the same early-career range as `research/format/cv_structure.md`. Include referees' names and emails only if the person supplied them, they agreed, and the page asks. Amsterdam's general list asks for two, with the relationship. [S17, D]
- A photo, date of birth, or marital status stays off unless the vacancy asks. The Dutch recruitment code tells organizations to request only what they need to judge suitability. [S24, D]
- **Own funding.** Do not submit a proposal until the match report records a supervisor who is at least in conversation, if the university requires agreement first. The proposal is `research_proposal.md`.
- A missing master's degree is a gap to surface, not a detail to bury, because these pages treat it as the normal requirement. [S17, D]
- No photo unless the vacancy asks. Grades stay on the original scale. Do not invent a Dutch grade equivalent.
