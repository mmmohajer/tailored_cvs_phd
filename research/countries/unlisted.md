# Countries without their own file

France, the Nordic countries, Australia, China, India, and many others do not yet have a graded file here. That is a gap in this folder, not permission to use the US file.

## What is known nearby

- **Biomedical programs in Sweden and Austria, in one small comparison, sat with the European pattern:** a master's-level degree was the usual way in, unlike the US bachelor's-to-umbrella path. The sites were Karolinska Institutet and Graz. [S15, B]
- **A review of admissions research names China alongside the US as a place where test scores are part of the file,** and names Germany alongside the UK as a place where a proposal can carry a humanities application. The review is not a substitute for those countries' own rules. [S14, B]
- **Australian project scholarships often tell the applicant to write to the supervisor before a formal form,** and to send a CV, transcripts, and a short account of interest. That instruction has been seen on individual university pages. It is not a finding that every Australian department works this way.

## What the agent does

The four files are always written. The essay follows the closest pattern below, and the match report says that pattern is unverified.

1. Classify the saved page as committee, supervisor-first, or advertised post (`which_system.md`).
2. Use the closest file only for the shape of the documents:
   - Committee, coursework, statement of purpose → `united_states.md`, and say the country itself is unverified.
   - Proposal plus a named supervisor, especially humanities → `united_kingdom.md` humanities section.
   - Vacancy for a paid doctoral researcher → `netherlands.md`.
   - Individual doctorate that needs a professor's yes first → `germany.md` individual route.
3. Write in the match report: "No country file for [country]. Closest pattern used: [file]. Confirm against the department page."
4. Ask the person when the page does not reveal the route. Do not guess a national exam, a photo rule, a visa rule, or a grade conversion.
5. If the person later brings a better source (a national admissions guide, a doctoral-school page), add it as the next source ID rather than silently promoting a guess to a rule.
