# Germany

Germany has two routes, and the file follows the route. Most of the detailed US evidence in this repo does not transfer.

## Findings

- **The national scholarship agency describes both an individual doctorate and a structured program.** An individual project is supervised by a university teacher. A structured program is a doctoral program the applicant joins. DAAD's own doctoral scholarships usually expect a master's degree or a Diplom, and a bachelor's only in exceptional cases. [S16, D]
- **That scholarship's document list is concrete:** a tabular CV of at most 3 pages, a publication list if there is one (at most 10 pages), and a letter of motivation of 1–2 pages giving academic and personal reasons for the project in Germany. [S16, D] This is one funder's list. A university job posting or a Graduiertenkolleg can ask for something else. The posting wins.
- **A proposal is weighty in some German humanities and social-science admissions, and less so in some STEM admissions.** That is reported in the UK study's review of earlier work, not from a Germany-wide sample in this folder. [S14, B]

## What the agent does

The four files are always written: custom CV, cover letter, email to one professor, and a follow-up plan. `motivation.md` is the country essay. On the individual route, `research_proposal.md` is added as well. The one-page cover letter does not replace either.

- **Individual route.** The match report states whether a supervisor has agreed. If they have not, the application is not finished: draft the documents the professor asked to see, and do not write as though a place exists. The research plan is `research_proposal.md`, limited to the person's actual project.
- **Structured program or advertised position.** Follow that call. It may look like a cohort application or like a job.
- **CV:** tabular, reverse chronological, at most 3 pages when the instruction is the DAAD list or the page is silent. [S16, D] Put publications in a separate list when they asked for one. Degrees and grades stay on the original scale.
- **Letter:** `motivation.md`, 1–2 pages when that is the requested genre. It gives the academic reason for this project and this place. It is not a US statement stretched to the same anecdotes. [S16, D]
- Do not add a photo, date of birth, or marital status unless the saved instructions ask. Silence is not a request. The German employment service says a photo is no longer required and is still common on job applications, and it suggests a two-page CV for that setting. [S23, D] A doctoral call or the DAAD three-page cap wins over that employment tip. [S16, D]
- Letters of recommendation follow the posting. A supervisor's confirmation of willingness is a different document from a reference, and the agent does not sign either.
