# Canada

Canadian doctoral admissions are not a copy of the US, and they are not one system. Thesis programs often want a supervisor identified before or during the application. Some departments still admit a cohort. The saved page decides which.

## Findings

- **In one biomedical comparison, the Canadian program sat closer to the European pattern than to the US umbrella.** At the University of Manitoba, the structure resembled European programs more than Vanderbilt's post-bachelor's umbrella. The study is four institutions, all biomedical. [S15, B]
- **There is no Canada-wide admissions survey in this folder** comparable to the CGS US figures or the Oxford and Cambridge interviews. Do not borrow US letter-versus-GRE percentages and call them Canadian.

## What the agent does

The four files are always written: custom CV, cover letter, email to one professor, and a follow-up plan. When the page is silent, also write `statement.md`. The cover letter does not replace it.

- Read the page for a required supervisor. If the application cannot be submitted without one, the match report says so, and the statement does not invent an agreement. Fit is "this person has invited a conversation" or "this person is listed as a possible supervisor," using only what the person and the page supply.
- If the page is a cohort program with rotations or a later match, follow `united_states.md` for the statement and say in the match report that this department is the committee route.
- CV: same academic shape as the US default (no photo, grades on the original scale, research before a skills dump) unless the department's instructions differ. One Toronto department caps the file at two single-spaced pages. A Canadian career centre says an academic CV has no fixed length. The silent default stays 1–2 pages for a first PhD application, and the match report says that range is not a national rule. [S21, D; S22, D]
- Letters: use the number on the page. If it is silent, three is the default, and the match report should say that default is not from a Canadian study.
- A master's is often expected and should be easy to see. If the person has only a bachelor's degree, check the department's minimum before treating the file as ready.
