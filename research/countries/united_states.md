# United States

Use this when the university is in the United States and its page does not specify another document set. The rest of `research/` was written for this system. Grades A and B there are hard rules here, and they are not automatic rules for other countries.

## Findings

- **A committee often screens, then reads.** Doctoral programs in the CGS survey named transcripts and GRE scores most often at the first screen, and letters and the personal or research statement most often at the final decision. [S06, B] Elite committees used grades, GRE, and undergraduate pedigree for the fast cut. [S07, B]
- **Many science programs admit after a bachelor's degree and choose the lab later.** At Vanderbilt's biomedical program, students entered an umbrella curriculum, rotated, and selected a mentor after that, with a qualifying exam around year two. [S15, B] This is not every US department. Engineering and some labs expect a named advisor in the application. Read the page.
- **The statement is for direction and fit, not for replacing the transcript.** [S05, A; S13, D]
- **GRE policy is local and has loosened.** In a 2025 survey of graduate-enrollment staff, the largest group said scores were neither required nor recommended in most or all programs. [S11, C]

## What the agent does

The four files are always written: custom CV, cover letter, email to one professor, and a follow-up plan. The rows below are the country default on top of those four. The page wins when it names a different set.

| Piece | Default when the page is silent |
|---|---|
| CV | 1–2 pages. Education, the matching research, and any papers on page one. GPA on the transcript's scale. No photo, age, or marital status. A third page only if the relevant record will not fit. [S18, D; S20, D] |
| Second document | `statement.md`, 700–1,000 words, qualifications, question, and fit. See `research/statement/` |
| Letters | Three writers who saw the work, unless the page sets another number. The agent does not write the letters |
| Tests | Include scores the person has only if the program considers them |
| Faculty | Name two or three whose work is in the materials. Do not write as if they have already accepted the person, unless a rotation program means the lab is chosen after arrival. In that case say so, and do not pretend a lab has been assigned |

A master's degree is an asset when the person has one. Do not treat its absence as a gap in a program that admits from a bachelor's degree. [S15, B]
