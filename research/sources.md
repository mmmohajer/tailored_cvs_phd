## Notes on bias

- S02, S03, S04, S09 come from companies that sell resume or parsing services. Their direction is useful; treat exact percentages loosely.
- S01's 7.4-second figure is the *initial* skim. It does not mean a resume is read for 7.4 seconds in total (S02 measured minutes in a simulation).
- S08 counts *detectable* ATS use. It shows ATSs are everywhere at large companies, not that they reject anyone (see S04).
