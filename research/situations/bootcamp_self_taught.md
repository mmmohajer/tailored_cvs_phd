# Bootcamp and self-taught

No computer science degree; skills from a bootcamp, online courses, or self-study.

## Findings

- Real hiring changes after degree requirements were dropped are concentrated in about 37% of firms ("Skills-Based Hiring Leaders"), which raised their share of non-BA hires by nearly 20%. [S14, B]
- Google's recruiters ask engineering applicants to link GitHub or open-source work. [S12, D]
- Employers weigh demonstrated competencies: 73% of employers that don't screen by GPA consider them. [S06, B]

## What the agent does

- Treat this as entry level (`../levels/entry_new_grad.md`), with Projects above any Experience that isn't technical.
- **Projects** are the main evidence: finished, deployed, documented, with links. Group work from a bootcamp is labeled as a team project and the person's own part is stated.
- **Open source:** even a few merged pull requests to real projects go in, with links.
- **Education and Training:** bootcamp name, program, and year; relevant courses and certificates. A degree in another field still goes in.
- **Skills:** only what the person can interview in (S12). Bootcamp curricula list many tools; keep the ones used in the person's projects.
- If the person has prior non-tech jobs, follow `career_switcher.md` for those.
- In `match_report.md`, note when the target company publicly hires without degrees or has a skills-based process; that is where S14 says the odds are real.
