# Experience and bullets

## Findings

- Recruiters look at job titles more than any other element, then company and dates, checking for steady progression. [S01, C]
- Bold titles over bulleted accomplishments performed best; short declarative statements beat paragraphs. [S01, C]
- Bock's formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]." Start with an active verb, measure the result, give a baseline, explain how. Specificity helps even for modest work. [S11, D]
- Google's recruiters repeat the same formula and ask for results and impact over long descriptions, in reverse chronological order with employer, title, and dates. [S12, D]
- Employers of new grads look for evidence of problem-solving (~90%), teamwork (~80%), and written communication, initiative, work ethic, and technical skills (70%+). [S07, B]

## What the agent does

**Each role**

`### Title | Company | City, Region | Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY` (or `Present`). Remote roles say `Remote`.

- Promotions at one company: list each title with its own dates under the same company, so progression is visible (S01).
- Contract or agency work: `Software Engineer (Contract) | Agency for Client | ...`.

**Bullets**

- Pattern: **verb + what + measurable result + how**. The result goes early in the line where possible.
- Numbers come only from the master. When a result has no number, use a concrete outcome instead (shipped, adopted by, replaced, unblocked), never an invented metric.
- Bullet budget per role, newest to oldest: 4–6, 3–4, 2–3, then 1–2. Roles older than ~10–12 years can collapse into one "Earlier experience" line for senior and staff resumes.
- Each bullet proves one thing the target job asks for. A bullet that proves nothing the job needs is cut first.
- Vary verbs. Avoid "responsible for," "helped with," "worked on," "utilized."
- Show teamwork and communication through actions (led a design review, wrote the runbook, mentored two interns) rather than adjectives (S07).
- Scope words must be true: "led" means the person led it; "owned" means they owned it. If the master says "contributed," the tailored version cannot say "led."

**Weak → strong (illustration)**

- Weak: "Responsible for improving API performance."
- Strong: "Cut p95 latency on the orders API from 900ms to 250ms by adding Redis caching and rewriting three N+1 queries."
