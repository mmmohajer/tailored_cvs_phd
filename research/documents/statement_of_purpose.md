# Statement of purpose

Written to `custom_resumes/<slug>/statement.md` only when that position asks for a statement of purpose or a research statement. It is in addition to the cover letter, not a replacement for it. Structure details are in `research/statement/`.

## Findings

- **At the final doctoral decision, the statement sits with the letters.** Programs named the personal or research statement as a top material (64%), just after letters (67%) and ahead of transcripts (55%). [S06, B]
- **It is a weak second transcript.** Across admissions studies, personal statements added no prediction of grades once test scores and prior grades were already in the model. [S05, A]
- **Readers use it for preparation, direction, and fit.** That is a faculty account, not a trial, and it matches what a grade-prediction study would miss: fit is not a GPA. [S13, D; S05, A]
- **The genre is "where I have worked, and why this program," with field-specific emphasis.** [S10, C]
- **Elite committees also notice who the applicant reminds them of.** Faculty described merit in terms that tracked their own training. Naming a famous lab without a real connection does not create fit, and copying the department's adjectives does not either. [S07, B]

## What the agent does

- Write one statement per program. The research question, the faculty named, and the reasons are specific to that department.
- Lead with a question or a line of work the person can actually do, then the preparation that supports it, then why this department is a place to pursue it. Details in `research/statement/structure.md`.
- Every project, method, grade, and paper traces to the master. The statement may interpret a true fact. It may not add a new one.
- Spend the letter on what the transcript cannot show: the question, how the person works, and fit. Do not retell the CV in paragraph form. [S05, A]
- One honest sentence on a real gap (a missing method, a thin research record) is allowed when the match report marks it as a gap and the person has a true way they are closing it. Do not apologize for a page.
- Faculty are named only when their work is in the program materials the person provided, and the overlap with the person's project is real. Otherwise leave a marked placeholder in "Edits for you." See `research/documents/fit.md`.
