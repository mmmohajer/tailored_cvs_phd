# Fit with the program and faculty

Who "fit" is for depends on the country. In many US programs a committee reads a statement and the lab is chosen later. In UK humanities, and on the German individual route, there is often no offer without a supervisor who can and will take the project. Read `research/countries/` before naming faculty. [S14, B; S16, D]

## Findings

- **Doctoral funding is often an advisor decision, not only a department decision.** CGS workshop notes on this survey record that when a professor's grant pays the student, that professor has strong control over who is admitted. The report's phrase for the underlying pattern, drawn from Posselt, is that "risk requires patrons." [S06, B]
- **Fit is also how committees reproduce themselves.** In elite programs, faculty recognized merit in applicants whose profiles looked like their own training. Fit is a real criterion and an easy place for a vague preference to hide. [S07, B]
- **The statement is a poor tool for predicting grades and a common tool for judging fit.** [S05, A; S13, D]
- **Disciplines disagree about what fitting in means.** A quantitative program and a text-based program are not scoring the same file. [S08, B]

## What the agent does

- Read the call, the faculty list, and any instructions saved in `positions/<slug>/`. The match report gets a short table: each stated interest or requirement, the master evidence, and `strong`, `partial`, or `gap`.
- Name two or three faculty only when both are true: their area is in the materials, and the person's project actually touches it. One sentence each on the overlap. No praise of the university's ranking, city, or "world-class faculty."
- If the person has not read anything by those faculty, the statement uses a placeholder ("[you: the paper or problem of Professor X you want to work on]") and "Edits for you" asks them to fill it. The agent does not invent a paper, a grant, or a lab technique.
- Do not tell the person they will be supervised by someone who has not agreed to it. "I hope to work with" is as far as the sentence goes.
- The professor email and the follow-up plan are standard files for every position. Write them with `research/documents/outreach.md`. The agent still does not send email, and the email still does not claim a place in the lab.
- When many must-haves are gaps (a required method, a required language, a required degree), say so plainly in the match report and let the person decide whether to apply.
