# Disciplinary differences

## Findings

- **Departments apply their own idea of merit.** In a comparative ethnography, faculty in high-consensus disciplines justified admissions standards from the field's theories, methods, and daily work. Economics and philosophy were not interchangeable. The study's claim is the pattern, not a universal rubric for every department. [S08, B]
- **The same numbers are not the goal in every field.** GRE and GPA predict graduate coursework across disciplines better than they predict publications. Subject tests predicted better than the general test. [S01, A] Biomedical committees that kept relying on the general GRE were using a score that, in two later single-university studies, did not track research productivity. [S02, B; S03, B]
- **Research duration has evidence behind it in STEM, not a matching study in the humanities in this folder.** [S09, B]
- **A writing sample carries weight where the dissertation is a long piece of prose.** That is a consequence of S08's finding that methods shape judgment, plus ordinary program instructions. Treat a program that requires a writing sample as having made this explicit.

## What the agent does

Read the program first. Use the defaults below only when it does not say what it weighs. Record the choice in `master/profile_facts.md` (for the person's field) and in the match report (for this program).

**Laboratory and computational sciences.** Lead the CV with research experience. State duration, advisor, method, and the person's role. [S09, B] Publications if real. Coursework in the methods the lab uses (statistics, a programming language, a bench technique) beats a skills list with no project attached.

**Quantitative social sciences (economics, quantitative political science, and programs that look like them).** Put the mathematical and statistical preparation where a first reader can see it: courses, a thesis method, a replication or an original empirical project. Do not hide a research paper behind a list of software.

**Humanities and other text-based fields.** The statement frames a question the person is ready to spend years on, in conversation with work they have actually read. If the program requires a writing sample, help the person choose among pieces they already wrote. Do not rewrite the sample into a different argument, and do not produce a fake chapter.

**Switching fields.** The bridge is a true project, course sequence, or job that used the new field's methods. Interest alone is a gap. See `research/situations/returning_and_industry.md`.
