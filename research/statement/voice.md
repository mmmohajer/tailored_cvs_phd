# Voice

## Findings

- **Generic statements are what the grade research says they are: almost no new information.** Once grades and tests are known, the statement did not improve prediction of grades. A paragraph that could be sent to any department of the same name is that kind of statement. [S05, A]
- **Specificity is the part readers describe wanting** (a direction, a reason for this program) and the part a small genre study found in successful files (reasons for applying). [S13, D; S10, C]
- **No study in this folder measures AI-written PhD statements against offers.** Do not borrow industry cover-letter experiments. The risk the evidence does support is a statement with no information beyond the CV.

## What the agent does

- Write in the person's concrete nouns: the dataset, the archive, the organism, the theorem, the classroom. Cut adjectives that any applicant could claim.
- First person is right for the statement. The CV stays without "I."
- Banned, and rewritten into a fact or deleted:
  - passionate about, ever since I was a child, this has been my dream, I have always been fascinated
  - I believe I would be a great fit, I am confident that I would contribute
  - world-class, cutting-edge, renowned faculty, utilize, leverage (as filler)
  - To whom it may concern
- "Edits for you" in the match report lists two to four places only the person can make true. Always include the faculty-fit sentences. The person should change those words before they submit. A statement nobody edited is a statement that may still be accurate and still sound like nobody in particular. [S05, A; S13, D]
