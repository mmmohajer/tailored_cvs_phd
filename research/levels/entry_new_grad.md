# Entry level / new grad

0–2 years of full-time experience, including recent graduates.

## Findings

- When two candidates are otherwise equal, employers say an internship with them or in their industry is the most influential factor. [S07, B]
- Employers use skills-based hiring at 70%; 73% of those that don't screen by GPA consider demonstrated proficiency in competencies. [S06, B]
- Two-page resumes had only a small advantage at entry level (1.4x) in a simulation where the two-page versions carried more content. [S02, C]
- Writing quality had its biggest hiring effect for new labor-market entrants with weaker writing. [S10, A]

## What the agent does

- **Length:** 1 page. A second page only when there are several substantial internships or shipped products, never to fit coursework.
- **Order:** Education first while within ~2 years of graduating, then Skills, Experience (internships first), Projects. After the first full-time job, Experience moves above Education.
- **Summary:** optional, 2 lines, only if it adds a target the history doesn't show.
- **Experience:** internships are written like real jobs: title, company, dates, 2–4 bullets with results.
- **Projects:** 2–3; they carry most of the evidence when internships are thin.
- **GPA:** include if 3.0+, drop after the first full-time role.
- **Proofreading:** non-negotiable [S10, A].
