# Internship

Applying for an internship or co-op while still in school.

## Findings

- Employers of students look for evidence of problem-solving (~90%), teamwork (~80%), and written communication, initiative, work ethic, and technical skills (70%+). [S07, B]
- Major, prior internships, and industry experience are the top factors; GPA screening is used by 42.1% of employers in 2026. [S06, B]
- Google: one page for internship roles; education first; projects, leadership, and extracurriculars welcome; GitHub linked. [S12, D]

## What the agent does

- **Length:** 1 page. [S12]
- **Order:** Header → Education → Skills → Projects → Experience → Leadership / Activities. Experience goes above Projects only when it is technical.
- **Education:** expected graduation date, GPA if 3.0+, 3–6 relevant courses.
- **Projects:** 2–3, each with a link and one number or concrete outcome (users, accuracy, latency, grade, hackathon placement).
- **Experience:** non-tech jobs count. Write them for transferable evidence of S07's attributes: "Trained 4 new hires on the POS system" shows teamwork and communication.
- **Summary:** skip it. Space goes to projects.
- **Cover letter:** write one when the application has a field for it (see `../cover_letter/when_and_why.md`).
