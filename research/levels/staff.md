# Staff / principal

Staff, Senior Staff, Principal. Technical leadership beyond one team.

## Findings

- Staff-plus roles cluster into four archetypes: **Tech Lead** (guides one team or a few teams' approach and execution), **Architect** (owns direction and quality of a critical technical area), **Solver** (digs into arbitrarily complex problems), **Right Hand** (extends an executive's attention across a large org). Companies emphasize different ones. [S16, D]
- Reilly frames staff work as big-picture thinking, execution of cross-team projects, and leveling up the engineers around you. [S17, D]
- Experienced candidates: two pages, with the second page earned by the first. [S01, C; S02, C]

No controlled study covers staff resumes specifically. Everything below is grade D, built on S16 and S17.

## What the agent does

- **Archetype first.** Decide which archetype the person and the target job match, from the master and the posting. The summary names that kind of work in plain words ("Staff engineer setting technical direction for payments infrastructure across four teams").
- **Length:** up to 2 pages (one full page is fine; never pad). Consider a "Selected impact" block of 3–4 lines on page one with organization-level results.
- **Bullets show organizational effect, not tasks:**
  - Strategy and direction: technical vision, architecture decisions with long-term effects, migrations across many teams.
  - Execution: multi-team or multi-quarter projects delivered, risk retired, incidents prevented.
  - Leverage: engineers mentored to promotion, standards or platforms adopted by N teams, hiring loops designed.
- **Credibility signals:** design docs and RFCs authored (titles, not links to private docs), talks, publications, patents, open-source maintainership.
- **Don't drown in old detail.** Roles older than ~12 years collapse into one line each.
- **No vague leadership claims** ("drove alignment"). Every leadership claim names what changed and for how many people or teams.
