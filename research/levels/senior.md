# Senior

About 5–10 years; titles like Senior Software Engineer.

## Findings

- Two-page resumes were preferred 2.9x for managerial-level roles and 2.6x for mid-level in a simulation. [S02, C]
- "The two-page rule remains for more experienced job seekers." Page two's reading time depends on page one; pages beyond two perform worse. [S01, C]
- No controlled study defines "senior" on a resume. Public engineering career ladders consistently describe it as owning outcomes beyond your own code: system design, technical decisions with trade-offs, raising the team through review and mentoring. [D]

## What the agent does

- **Length:** 2 pages. Page one holds the summary, skills, and the most relevant 1–2 roles.
- **Order:** Header → Summary → Skills → Experience → Education (projects only if notable).
- **Bullets show scope and judgment:** systems designed and owned, trade-offs made and why, incidents led, cross-team work, people mentored and what they went on to do, business results.
- **Older roles:** compress. Jobs older than ~10 years get 1–2 bullets or an "Earlier experience" line.
- **Titles:** if a company's title understates the work (for example "Engineer II" doing senior work), keep the real title and let bullets show scope. Never change a title.
