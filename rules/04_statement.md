# Cover letter, professor email, and follow-up

Written in the same tailoring run as the custom CV, into `custom_resumes/<slug>/`.

## Read first

`research/countries/which_system.md`, the country file, `research/documents/fit.md`, `research/documents/outreach.md`, `research/statement/structure.md`, and `research/statement/voice.md`.

The cover letter is always written. It does not replace the essay that country expects. When `positions/<slug>/` names the essays, write those and skip the country default. When the page is silent, also write the country default in `research/statement/which_essay.md` (a statement, a research proposal, or a motivation letter). Do not add a diversity statement, a teaching statement, or a second proposal the page did not request.

## Cover letter → `cover_letter.md`

1. Use `renderer/templates/cover_letter.md`. Address the committee, or the named contact, as the call does.
2. One proof story from `master/stories.md` that matches this position. Do not retell the whole CV.
3. Name faculty only when their work is in the position folder and the overlap is real. Otherwise use a placeholder and list it under "Edits for you."
4. About 250–400 words and one page, unless the call sets another length.
5. Every number and claim traces to the master. Do not promise a supervisor who has not agreed.

## Email → `professor_email.md`

Follow `research/documents/outreach.md` and `renderer/templates/professor_email.md`. One professor. 120–180 words. The subject names the vacancy or the research area. The gap stays in the body. The agent does not send it.

## Follow-up → `follow_up.md`

Follow `research/documents/outreach.md` and `renderer/templates/follow_up.md`. Include a draft of the single reminder. No invented reply and no second reminder, except the deadline exception in that file.

## Edits for you

Add 2–4 notes to `match_report.md`. Always include the sentence that depends on a paper or problem only the person has read.
