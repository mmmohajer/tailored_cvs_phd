# Stories

## 1. Claims search at Maplecrest

- **Situation:** Adjusters clicked through three screens to find one claim.
- **What I did:** Built a search page in React.js with a Node.js/Express API, and wrote 40 Jest tests for it.
- **Result:** Lookups went from about 5 minutes to under 1 minute (supervisor's estimate). Demoed to the claims team of about 20 people.
- **Proves:** React.js, REST APIs, testing, working with real users.

## 2. StudySpot

- **Situation:** Students wandered the library during exams looking for an empty room.
- **What I did:** In a team of 3, built the Next.js frontend and the job that syncs the room booking feed into PostgreSQL.
- **Result:** About 600 students used it in the April 2025 exam period; 2nd place of 45 teams at Northgate Hacks 2025.
- **Proves:** shipping under a deadline, TypeScript, SQL, real users.

## 3. Teaching 30 students a week

- **Situation:** Intro programming students got stuck on the same few ideas every week.
- **What I did:** Ran the weekly lab and office hours twice a week.
- **Result:** 30 students a week, Jan 2025 – Apr 2026.
- **Proves:** explaining technical ideas clearly, patience, communication.
