Lumen Pay — Backend Engineer, Payments Platform
Toronto, ON (hybrid, 2 days in office)

About Lumen Pay
Lumen Pay helps independent clinics, salons, and studios get paid: online booking deposits, card payments, and automatic payouts. We process payments for 12,000 small businesses across Canada.

The role
You'll join the Payments Platform team, which owns the services that move money: payment intents, refunds, payouts, and the ledger that keeps it all correct. We care about correctness first, then speed.

What you'll do
- Design, build, and operate Python services for payments and payouts
- Build reliable asynchronous workflows (retries, idempotency, exactly-once effects)
- Evolve our PostgreSQL schemas safely as we grow
- Improve observability and take part in our on-call rotation
- Review code and help newer engineers grow

What we're looking for
- 3+ years of professional backend experience with Python
- Production experience with Django or FastAPI
- Strong PostgreSQL skills, including migrations on live systems
- Experience with distributed task queues or event streaming (Celery, Kafka, or similar)
- Experience with AWS
- A strong testing culture: you write tests as part of the work

Nice to have
- Payments, fintech, or ledger experience
- Kafka
- Terraform or other infrastructure as code
- Experience in health or wellness software

How to apply
Send your resume and a short cover letter telling us why Lumen Pay.
