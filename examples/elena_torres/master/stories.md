# Stories

## 1. The grading script that spread

- **Situation:** Grading weekly quizzes took about 6 hours of every week.
- **What I did:** Wrote a Python tool on the Google Sheets API that grades Google Forms quizzes and emails each student their feedback.
- **Result:** About 1 hour a week instead of about 6; 5 other math teachers started using it.
- **Proves:** automation, APIs, building something colleagues adopted.

## 2. QuizLoop

- **Situation:** Bootcamp final project, aimed at the same grading problem.
- **What I did:** Built QuizLoop's Django REST Framework backend and grading logic with one teammate on the React.js UI; 85% test coverage with pytest; deployed on Render.
- **Result:** 3 teachers piloted it with about 90 students in spring 2026.
- **Proves:** Django, REST APIs, testing, shipping to real users, the domain knowledge ed-tech needs.

## 3. Summit Tutoring bookings

- **Situation:** A tutoring centre took every booking by phone.
- **What I did:** Built their website and booking form (Next.js, a small Django API, PostgreSQL).
- **Result:** About 40 bookings a month now come through the site.
- **Proves:** full-stack delivery for a paying client, end to end.

## 4. Grade 9 pass rates

- **Situation:** Too many grade 9 applied math students were failing.
- **What I did:** Used quiz data to design a grouping plan for extra help; the whole department ran it.
- **Result:** Pass rate rose from 68% to 81% over two school years (department report).
- **Proves:** using data to change outcomes, working across a team.
