Quillstack — Staff Software Engineer, Developer Platform
Remote (Canada) or Toronto

About Quillstack
Quillstack builds accounting software for mid-sized businesses. Our engineering org is 180 engineers across 30 teams, and it's growing.

The role
Our developer platform is a patchwork: three ways to deploy, two CI systems, and a lot of tribal knowledge. We're hiring our first Staff engineer for the platform to set its direction and lead the work to make shipping safe and boring for every team.

What you'll do
- Set the multi-year technical direction for the developer platform
- Lead multi-team migrations (CI consolidation, moving remaining services to Kubernetes)
- Write and review RFCs; help run architecture review
- Raise the bar on reliability: ownership, on-call, incident review
- Mentor senior engineers and help grow future Staff engineers

What we're looking for
- 10+ years in software engineering, with recent Staff-level scope
- Deep Kubernetes experience in production
- Terraform or other infrastructure as code
- Experience building CI/CD platforms used by many teams
- A record of leading migrations across many teams
- Strong written communication (RFCs, design docs)
- AWS

Nice to have
- Go or Python
- OpenTelemetry
- Backstage or another developer portal
- Fintech or accounting domain

How to apply
Resume and cover letter. Tell us about a migration you led and what you'd do differently.
